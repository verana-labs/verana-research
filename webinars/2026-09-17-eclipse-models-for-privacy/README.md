# Why AI Agents Must Have Their Own Identity?

**Eclipse Models for Privacy Interest Group webinar · September 17, 2026**

Speakers: Fabrice Rochette (co-founder, The Verana Foundation) and Ariel Gentile (CTO, 2060 OÜ). Hosted by the [Eclipse Models for Privacy](https://models4privacy.org/) Interest Group, with Trialog.

The talk argues that AI agents need their own verifiable identity, proving three things no user account can: an identifier the agent controls, an accountable operator, and ecosystem accreditations, and that this identity must be verifiable by anyone while observed by no one. It surveys the September 2026 agent-identity landscape, shows why a central agent registry fails privacy threat modeling (LINDDUN: linking, detecting, data disclosure), and presents Verifiable Trust as one shared, privacy-preserving trust layer for services, organizations, IoT devices and AI agents alike.
