# Governing a Registry of Registries: Verana's Layered Governance

Talk given at the **Trust over IP Foundation, Ecosystem & Governance Working Group (EGWG)** speaker series on **October 1, 2026**.

- Speaker: Fabrice Rochette, Co-founder, Verana Foundation; CEO, 2060
- Subtitle: Separation of powers, enumerated authority and autonomous ecosystems
- Format: 25-30 minute talk, 15 minute Q&A; recorded by ToIP and published on the LF Digital Trust YouTube channel

## Files

- `verana-governance-toip-egwg-v2.html`: the slide deck as presented (v2, 18 slides). Part one explains what Verana is with the three verana.io bricks (ecosystems, identity, discovery) and a one-slide summary illustrated by a live Proof of Trust; part two covers governance: the two entities, the layered model, then the Network Governance Framework chapter by chapter.
- `verana-governance-toip-egwg-v2.pdf`: the same deck as a 16:9 PDF, one slide per page.
- `verana-governance-toip-egwg-v1.html`: the first cut (16 slides), kept for reference.

Open either file in a browser; arrow keys or space to navigate, `N` toggles speaker notes, `D` toggles dark/light, `F` fullscreen. Print to PDF gives one slide per page.

## What the talk covers

The Verana Network Governance Framework (DRAFT 0.4) as presented to the working group: the separation between the Verana Foundation (specifications, software), the Verana Council (network governance and security) and autonomous ecosystems; the Council's enumerated on-chain powers and hard limits; the one-member-one-vote council with denominator discipline; federalism and the recognition (not approval) of ecosystem governance frameworks; economics as governance invariants; sanctions, legal orders and the graduated ladder; evolution, the emergency fast path and AGPL as a condition of validator service; transition and steward sunsets; and the open `[DECISION]` flags put to the working group.

Everything shown describes a **draft**, written for co-authoring by the Founding Members of the Verana Council Association and ratified only at its incorporation General Assembly (target Q4 2026).
